| A2-D1 | build-doc §10.2 `cascade_emerges` | **"mesopredator count strictly rising within 4 seasons" is not achievable per-run at any population scale the shipped content contains, and in the MAJORITY of successful cascades the mesopredator does not survive at all.** An apex starves only while `availablePrey / demand < starveThreshold`, i.e. while its whole prey pool is under `starveThreshold × demandPerIndividual.Apex = 0.4 × 6 = 2.4` prey **per surviving apex**. As the apex dies its demand shrinks with it, so the *last* apex individual only starves while the entire pool is below 2.4 animals — and the mesopredator is normally inside that pool. Measured over the generated property and over the shipped nodes: a released mesopredator is never more than 2 individuals, 2 individuals cannot produce a whole individual in one season at any breed rate the data contemplates (shipped mesopredator rate 0.045; the highest rate anywhere is `waterTableBreedFloor` 0.15 ⇒ ≤ 0.375 births/season), and stochastic rounding (§7.3 step 4) then makes a strict integer rise a coin flip. At `r4`, the one shipped node where release is structurally possible, the strict rise fires in **16%** of seeds at 4 seasons and 39% at 16. | **Not weakened, restated at the strength that is true, with the failure mode counted rather than hidden.** `tests/property/ecosim.test.ts::cascade_emerges` asserts as per-run LAWS: (1) a starving population strictly declines every season and never breeds; (2) the apex reaches 0 within `cascade.apexCollapseSeasons`; (3) extinction is permanent; (4) **release** — every line the dead apex used to eat takes exactly zero predation from it thereafter and never declines in a season it is not starving; (5) **"a new apex risen"** — nothing at the node preys on the mesopredator any more, asserted **whether or not the mesopredator survived** (the previous `count > 0` guard is what let this clause go unexecuted at five of six shipped nodes). The *rise* is asserted in aggregate over **every** successful cascade, counting a co-extinct mesopredator as the 0 it is — the earlier version averaged over survivors only, which is a survivorship-biased mean. **Blocker for A1/A5, not fixable in A2:** making the integer rise a per-run law needs either a release-scale `breedRate` on mesopredator lines in `data/nodes/*.json` or larger shipped counts; both are A1-owned and neither was changed. |
