## A1 — MOVE THE DEMO CASCADE FROM r4 TO r2. Highest priority.

**The problem.** `fixtures/overcatch-script.json` reproduces its recorded trace exactly, so the
test is green — but green only proves the replay is deterministic at one seed, not that the beat
is robust. Re-running the fixture's own protocol shape at **r4** over 128 seeds:

| | r4 (current) | r2 (proposed) |
|---|---|---|
| apex → 0 | 100% | **100%** |
| throne refilled by a new apex | 61% | **98%** |
| new apex strictly rising | 13% | **43%** |
| over-caught tier co-extincts (beat ruined) | **95%** | **2%** |
| complete beat lands | **0%** | — |

The fixture's own `_why` claims the beat "holds for a majority of low seeds". Measured: **3 of 64**.
Seed 12 works because a single stochastic rounding went its way — the prey sits at 7.09 expected and
needs the ~9% branch.

**Why r2 is strictly better**, beyond the numbers: r2 (Brackmarsh, Fenmaw over Reedrick + Mirefin) is
a **vertical-slice node**, so the demo no longer depends on a node that may ship as a vignette card.
This also closes the open risk recorded in `LANE-A-STATUS.md` §6.2, which had the slice beat as
"not demonstrated".

**Action:** author the over-catch action log against r2, keep the r4 one as a secondary fixture, and
point `cascade_emerges` and the demo-recording flow at r2. Do NOT tune the sim to rescue r4 (G6).

## A2 — Gym 1 is the hardest gym in the game. The difficulty curve is inverted.

500 seeds/gym, player at level parity with the leader's rank and file:

| | Maribel | Odo | Vesper | Sable | Noct | Greaves |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| win rate | **26.0%** | 66.0% | 56.8% | 59.8% | 60.6% | 36.2% |
| §10.5 target | 80–95 | 80–95 | 60–80 | 60–80 | 45–65 | 45–65 |

**1 of 6 in band.** At the untrained level curve it is 0 of 6, with Maribel at **0.2%**.
Not a type-coverage failure — the drafted party covers 3.8 of Maribel's 4 super-effectively.

Measured controls that make the number readable: a mirror match (leader's own team on the player's
side) wins 93–100%, so the player *seat* is worth ~+46pp and is already included above.
`encounter.ageWeights` is worth +19.2pp. A full 6-party vs a matched 4 is worth +40.8pp.

**Action:** the two INV-3-legal levers get partway there — `encounter.ageWeights` → +21.5pp mean
(Maribel 26 → 50, still short), `encounter.levelByYear` → +7.8pp untrained. Closing the rest needs
**gym levels**, which are an ASSUMPTION made during transcription (bible §7 gives none), not a
balance tunable. That is a content edit and needs a decision.

## A3 — Instinct is nearly a dead stat, and the specs disagree about it. NEEDS HANK.

Instinct is the **strongest single predictor of win rate across the roster, and it is NEGATIVE**
(r = −0.582). Cause: bible §2 gives Instinct three jobs — "crit chance, bait success, sign-reading
quality" — but `core/battle.ts` uses it for crit only, and `catchChance` never reads it at all. Over
the roster's 40-point Instinct spread, crit buys ~2.9% expected damage. So every line that invested
in Instinct is strictly worse for it.

This is a **spec conflict, not a bug**: build-doc §7.4's inlined catch formula
(`p = baseCatch × snareTier × baitMult × (2 − hpFrac)/2 × lureMult`) has no Instinct term, and the
build doc's audience note says an inlined formula wins over the bible. The implementation followed
the rule correctly. But the result is that one of five stats does ~nothing, which is a real design
cost the doc conflict hides.

**Action:** Hank's call. Either (a) amend build-doc §7.4 to give bait success an Instinct term, which
restores the bible's intent and makes the stat live, or (b) accept it and amend bible §2's
description so the stat is not oversold. Logged, not silently fixed (G2).

## A4 — Base stats measure allocation, not quality (informational, no action)

Every line spends exactly 300 base-stat points (`_statRule`), so base-stat total correlates with win
rate at r = 0.000, exactly as it must. 10 of 24 non-Ancient lines fall outside the 35–65% band —
dominant: Bramblore 79%, Mirefin 77%, Gloomoth 73%. Weak: Sporantle 13%, **Carricrow 7.9%**.

**59% of matchups are decided outright** (one side wins all 64 seeds), which rules out rescaling the
`types` group as a fix — rescaling a decided matchup only changes how fast the loser loses. The tail
needs `baseStats` edits, which are content.

## A5 — Emergent collapse with no player at all (informational — and good)

The six-year baseline with **no player** shows 2 extinctions at r4. One is the Cullbrand raid target
(Cragmar, scripted). The other is **Stonewing, the apex, starving out in year 5 with nothing
scripting it** — the raid harvests grazers only and the collapse above it emerges. That is G6 working
exactly as intended and it is strong interview material.

It does however sit against §10.5 mode (c)'s "no unintended extinction under normal play". Decide
whether an unattended apex collapse counts as intended.
