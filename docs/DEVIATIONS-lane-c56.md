# Deviations — lane C5 + C6 (season meter, season tint, year turn, retirement)

Files: `game/ui/SeasonMeter.ts`, `game/season-tint.ts`, `game/year-events.ts`,
`game/scenes/YearTurnScene.ts`, `game/scenes/RetirementScene.ts`, `game/scene-keys.ts`,
plus the wiring in `game/main.ts`, `game/boot.ts` and `game/scenes/OverworldScene.ts`.

Every row below is a departure from a document, or a decision a document does not make. Nothing here
is a silent change. Per build-doc §16 G12, no row states effort in human engineering-hours.

| # | Spec ref | Deviation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| C56-D1 | build-doc §7.5 steps 1–6 vs build-doc §4 step 5 | The year-turn beats play in **§7.5's numbered order**: festival → aging → unaged Ancients → form changes → **retirement flow** → raid ledger → map year-variant swap. §4 step 5 lists the same six items with the map swap **ahead of** the Cullbrand raid scripts. | The two build-doc passages disagree about raid-vs-map-swap order. §7.5 is the one that *numbers* the steps and gives the retirement flow step (3), so it is the inlined assignment and it wins (build doc, Audience note). The order matters for more than presentation: the prompts are now **answered at the retirement beat**, not after every beat has played, so the beats that follow it are read against a party that has already been resolved. |
| C56-D2 | build-doc §4 "the year turn runs" / core/lifecycle.ts `canRunYearTurn` | `driveYearTurn` **requires** `endedYear` and refuses unless `save.year === endedYear`. | `canRunYearTurn` is keyed on the year the turn will BEGIN, which is the right question for core and the wrong one for a shell with §4's two triggers. Measured: after one turn, `save.year` has already advanced, so a second call with the post-turn save passed the check and ran a whole extra year — two festivals, two aging passes, two season steps, two retirement rounds and two raid applications from one player action. Naming the ended year makes the second fire structurally impossible. No change to core. |
| C56-D3 | core/clock.ts (`advance`: "ticks beyond the boundary carry into the new year"), core/lifecycle.ts `runYearTurn` (`tick: balance.clock.startTick`) | `finishYearTurn(result, tickBefore, carryTick)` restores the tick the clock carried into the new year. The action-log entries still carry the tick the turn *started* on, exactly as `yearTurnActions` writes them. | `runYearTurn` resets the meter because it has no clock; the shell has one. Measured before the fix: an inn taken on tick 15 of a 16-tick year committed Y2 tick 0 while this lane's own `previewAction` — which runs the REAL transition — promised Y2 tick 1. Both halves of that seam are C56's, so C56 reconciles them. **Known limit:** `core/save.ts` `replay` does not model the clock at all (travel and foray do not move the tick on the replay path either), so a replayed save's `tick` field already differed from a live one before this change. This does not widen that gap; closing it is a core/save.ts question. |
| C56-D4 | build-doc §7.5 step 3; core/lifecycle.ts `advanceYear` | A retirement prompt whose **offered choice and rolled-HEIR fallback are both illegal** is recorded twice (`choiceRejected` ×2) and **passed over** — the retiree is gone and nothing replaces it. | `advanceYear` substitutes the rolled HEIR without re-checking it, so a doubly-illegal prompt reached `resolveRetirement` and threw — discarding a year turn that had already run its season step, its scripted raid and its map swap, with the retiree removed and the seeded stream advanced. That is precisely the outcome `advanceYear`'s own fallback exists to avoid. The state is reachable (party and box both full **and** every survivor at `retirement.boonMaxStacksPerStat` on every stat) and is constructed in the suite. Skipping is the only non-crashing outcome that states the truth. |
| C56-D5 | Hard Req 6 / bible §12a ("summer: +brightness") | `balance.audio.seasonBrightness > 1` **desaturates toward white** instead of multiplying. | Phaser's `setTint` is a per-channel MULTIPLY: there is no colour that draws the painted map brighter than it was painted. Measured: summer's 1.15 pushed r 255→293 and g 224→258, both clamped, turning a 255:224 red:green ratio into 1:1 — a hue change dressed up as a brightening. Spending the surplus on desaturation is the only reading a multiply can honour, and it means "summer is the season the map is least tinted". |
| C56-D6 | bible §12a ("year aging progressively darkens") | The season preset is split into a **hue** (normalised so its brightest channel is 255) and a **luminance** (`min(brightness,1) × yearDarkening`). The reading's brightest channel is exactly `255 × scale`. | The authored preset is itself a multiplier below 255 on most channels, so the old reading stacked THREE dimming terms where the spec names two. Measured on shipped balance: winter year 6 came out rgb(68, 84, 102) — 27% of the painted map's own brightness — where 0.75 × 0.60 asks for 45%. The hues remain `provisional` placeholders (see the blocker below); what changed is that they no longer secretly carry brightness. |
| C56-D7 | bible §9 "Cullbrand Harvest raids (scripted per act)" | `raidCoverage` reports the unauthored acts over `raidableYears` = `startYear+1 … finalYear` (2..6 on the shipped clock), not over all six years, and adds `unreachableScriptedYears`. | `runYearTurn` computes `newYear = save.year + 1` and applies only raids whose year equals it, so **no turn can fire a raid in the year the player starts in**, and the turn out of the final year begins a year `core/clock.ts` `isOver` calls past the game. Reporting `startYear` as an unauthored act promised a beat the engine cannot produce. `unreachableScriptedYears` is the mirror-image failure: a raid authored for year 1 or year 7 would silently never happen. |
| C56-D8 | build-doc §7.6 / `game/boot.ts` G11 note | The two scene style blocks read `balance.map.virtualWidth`/`virtualHeight` through `defaultYearTurnStyle(balance)` / `defaultRetirementStyle(balance)` instead of `width: 480, height: 320`. | Every other scene in the repo already complied; nothing caught these because `tests/gates/guardrails.test.ts` scopes `no_magic_numbers` to `core/`. A repo-wide `game/` gate is a shared-file change and is reported as a blocker rather than taken here. |
| C56-D9 | `game/boot.ts` SCENE_KEYS | The scene-key registry and `sceneRegistered` moved to a new engine-free module `game/scene-keys.ts`; `game/boot.ts` re-exports both unchanged. `YEAR_TURN_SCENE_KEY`/`RETIREMENT_SCENE_KEY` are now `SCENE_KEYS.yearTurn`/`.retirement`. | The registry's stated purpose is "nobody invents a second spelling", and the two scenes had invented `"year-turn"` and `"retirement"` — neither in the object nor in its PascalCase convention, so `sceneRegistered(scene, SCENE_KEYS.*)` could not reach them. ## Blockers reported out of this lane

1. **No `seasonTint` group in balance.json.** The four season hues are `PROVISIONAL_SEASON_TINTS` in
   `game/season-tint.ts`, flagged `provisional: true` and said so on screen. `zBalance` is a plain
   `z.object` and strips undeclared keys, so adding the group to the file alone would be a decoy no
   reader ever sees; it needs data/balance.json + `Balance` + `zBalance` in one change. The
   *magnitudes* (`audio.seasonBrightness`, `audio.yearDarkenPerYear`) are already data and are read.
2. **No `lifecycle` subsystem RNG tag** (C56-D10).
3. **No `no_magic_numbers` gate over `game/`** (C56-D8). The four named gates in
   `tests/gates/guardrails.test.ts` are scoped to `core/`, so nothing in the tree would have caught
   the two literal viewport sizes. Extending the gate touches a shared gate file.
4. **A new game seeds an empty party.** `startNewGame` takes an optional `party` and boot does not
   invent one (correctly — G2; build-doc §15 C7 owns the opening beat). Until C7 hands a starter
   over, a year turn reached by playing has nothing to age and nothing to retire, so the Legacy beat
   is *wired and reachable* but has no subject in a fresh run.
