# AUTHORED LAYOUT for "hearthollow" — this file IS the map. Composed cell by cell, by hand.
#
# The maps are stamped from these characters, one tile per cell, so the painting and the
# collision mask are the same document by construction. Nothing downstream invents geometry:
# where a building stands here is where it stands on screen, and a lane that stops in a field
# here stops in a field on screen. Re-running tools/mapgen/seed.ts WOULD OVERWRITE this; the
# seeder knows the adjacency and the prop coordinates and nothing else about the place.
#
# ── coordinate space ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# space        map-native pixels, origin TOP-LEFT (data/nodes/*.json props[].x/y are in this space)
# grid         64 cols × 64 rows, one character per collision cell
# cell         16 native px  (balance.map.collisionCellPx; 1024/64 = 16)
# map          1024 × 1024 native px, rendered ×2 → 2048 world px
# tiles        64 × 64 movement tiles of 32 world px
# col ← x      col = floor(nativeX / 16);  a cell's centre is native (16·col + 8, 16·row + 8)
# centre       native (512, 512) = cell (32, 32) = the tile firstWalkableTile starts on
# screen       the player sees 240×160 native at once = 15 × 10 CELLS
#
# ── authored from ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# biome        Meadow
# adjacency    r1
# bottom edge  — sealed; this is the head of the chain
# top edge     r1, ONE mouth, cells (40,0)-(42,0), ^ at (41,0) — the timber field gate,
#               which now stands on r1's bottom mouth (also col 41)
# settlement   yes — five buildings, each a place tools/comfy/manifest.json _nodes.hearthollow names
# props        hearthollow-signpost @ (512,648) → cell (32,40), unmoved
#
# ── what this place is ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# A farming hamlet in a wooded hollow. One open grazing green fills the middle; the five
# buildings stand well apart around and below it; a worn dirt lane comes down out of the north
# wood through the field gate, dog-legs past the supply shop, and forks on the green. The
# south-west quarter is farm: a fallow west field, a deep-thatched cottage, a thick hedgerow
# behind it, and the standing barley — the only ground in town where a wild thing turns up.
#
#   (34, 6)-(40,12)  SUPPLY SHOP        Kett's — snares, salve, tonic, the open bait crate
#   ( 9,17)-(16,23)  INN                with its byre yard railed off at the east end
#   (44,22)-(51,28)  FIELD STATION      Warden Maren's, the specimen pen below, drying racks east
#   (10,38)-(16,44)  COTTAGE            Tolm's, between the fallow west field and the barley
#   (32,43)-(39,50)  GYM HALL           Maribel's, at the foot of the green, shed yard to the east
#
# Every door is on the building's SOUTH face and every one of them is the end of a lane.
#
# ── decisions this file made ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
# · THE PLACE. A farming hamlet in a wooded hollow: one lopsided grazing green as the
#   organising void, the farm quarter filling the south-west, and the lane forking rather
#   than crossing — this is the head of the chain and has one mouth, so no lane crosses the
#   map. Cell (32,32) is open cropped turf one step from the fork.
# · THE FIVE BUILDINGS, all solid rectangles inside the shipped 85-140px footprint band, all
#   five a different size and shape, every door on the south face and reachable:
#     supply shop      (35,7)-(40,12)   6x6 = 96x96    door (37,13)
#     inn              (9,18)-(16,23)   8x6 = 128x96   door (13,24)   long and low
#     field station    (45,21)-(50,28)  6x8 = 96x128   door (47,29)   deep
#     cottage          (10,39)-(16,44)  7x6 = 112x96   door (13,45)
#     gym hall         (32,43)-(39,50)  8x8 = 128x128  door (36,51)   the largest
# · THREE RAILED YARDS OF THREE DIFFERENT SHAPES carry the silhouette variety the footprint
#   band cannot: a long shallow byre yard east of the inn, the big specimen pen below the
#   field station, a tall narrow shed yard beside the gym. Drying racks are two short rails.
# · THE TIMBER FIELD GATE the subject line names now stands at the mouth, in "F" posts. It
#   was missing, while r1 drew its own boundary wall on the other side of the same crossing.
# · THE TOP MOUTH IS AT COL 41, sitting exactly on r1's bottom mouth, so the track does not
#   snap sideways at the seam.
# · ENCOUNTER TERRAIN. 5.5% of walkable, one patch: the standing barley behind the hedgerow
#   in the farm quarter. A town is safe ground; this is the one place the content puts a wild
#   thing (Maribel's line has one of hers "through the hedge into the barley").
#
# ── legend (tools/mapgen/legend.ts) ──────────────────────────────────────────
#   .  floor  open-ground         plain walkable ground; the default fill
#   ,  floor  scrub               walkable but textured — long grass, ferns, heather
#   :  floor  path-corridor       the worn track that joins the map edges through the middle
#   o  floor  centre-clearing     the open region at native (512,512) the spawn search lands in
#   ~  floor  shallow             walkable water margin — wadeable, not a wall
#   #  WALL   wall                generic impassable mass with no more specific kind
#   B  WALL   building-footprint  a building the player walks around, never through
#   T  WALL   tree-mass           trunks and thicket dense enough to stop a walk
#   W  WALL   water               open water — deep enough to block
#   R  WALL   rock                basalt, scree, standing stone, crater rim
#   F  WALL   fence               a thin built barrier — pen rail, jetty edge, ore-cart rail
#   *  ANCH   prop-anchor         a data/nodes/*.json props[] entry, at floor(x/cellPx), floor(y/cellPx)
#   @  ANCH   spawn-anchor        documentary marker for the centre cell the spawn search starts from
#   ^  ANCH   edge-mouth          the cell where a corridor meets the map edge; one per declared adjacency
#   D  ANCH   door-anchor         the walkable cell a building is entered from
#
# In this map: 'o' is the close-cropped turf of the green, '.' is the packed brown earth of the
# yards, aprons and the fallow field, ',' is the standing barley and nothing else, 'T' is the
# oak wood and the hedgerow, 'F' is pen rail and field rail, 'R' is the hollow's bare stone.
#
# Only "#"-class characters block. tools/mapgen/layout.ts normalises every WALL character to "#"
# before game/collision.ts gridFromAscii sees it; ANCH characters parse as floor and are read again
# on a second pass. Region kinds are open strings — add a row to the legend, not a case to a switch.
