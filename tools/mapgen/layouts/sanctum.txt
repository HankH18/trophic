# HAND-AUTHORED MAP DESIGN for "sanctum". This file IS the map.
#
# It began as the seeded starting layout written by tools/mapgen/seed.ts and the grid below the
# "---" has since been redesigned by hand. The seeder knows four things about this node: which
# edges its adjacency declares, where its props stand, what biome it is, and whether it is a
# settlement. It does not know what this place looks like. RE-RUNNING THE SEEDER OVER THIS FILE
# WOULD DESTROY THE DESIGN; the renderers and the tile composer read this file, not the seeder.
#
# ── coordinate space ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# space        map-native pixels, origin TOP-LEFT (data/nodes/*.json props[].x/y are in this space)
# grid         64 cols × 64 rows, one character per collision cell
# cell         16 native px  (balance.map.collisionCellPx; 1024/64 = 16)
# map          1024 × 1024 native px, rendered ×2 → 2048 world px
# tiles        64 × 64 movement tiles of 32 world px
# col ← x      col = floor(nativeX / 16);  a cell's centre is native (16·col + 8, 16·row + 8)
# centre       native (512, 512) = cell (32, 32) = the tile firstWalkableTile starts on
# screen       the player sees 240×160 native at once = 15 × 10 CELLS
#
# ── seeded from ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# biome        Sanctum
# adjacency    sanctum-approach
# bottom edge  sanctum-approach — ^ at (20,63), on the approach's cleft
# top edge     — sealed; this is the terminus
# settlement   no — zero building footprints
# props        sanctum-antler-ring @ (512,512) → cell (32,32)
#
# ── what this place is (authored; nothing here was invented) ─────────────────
# tools/comfy/manifest.json _nodes.sanctum, verbatim in substance: a sealed caldera with a wide
# flat floor of short grazed grass and pale volcanic grit; A RING OF NINE TALL GREY STANDING
# STONES at the middle of the picture, evenly spaced, every one facing in toward the centre;
# inside the ring the grass is grazed short and even and THE EXACT MIDDLE IS OPEN GRAZED GRASS
# WITH NOTHING STANDING ON IT, while outside the ring the grass stands knee high everywhere;
# fallen slabs, dry heather and a few dead trees on the floor around it; ONE worn footpath
# climbing in AT THE BOTTOM through a tall gate standing open in a face of cut dark rock, coming
# up to the ring and entering it through a gap between two stones.
# data/dialogue.json sanctum.vellsire.approach adds the one piece of topography: "the stag comes
# down into the ring from the high side of the caldera" — so the rim is HIGH on the north side and
# steps down in shelves, with a grass tongue between them.
#
# ── decisions this file made ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
# · THIS NODE IS THE TERMINUS SITE AND CARRIES NO BUILDING. The subject line names none;
#   content-bible §6 gives the Sanctum "no shops"; data/interiors/ holds no sanctum file; and
#   checkSettlementOnly fails any footprint on a non-settlement. A round brief that asked for four
#   to seven buildings here was wrong about the node. The one structure on the map is the ring of
#   nine standing stones, drawn in "R" at radius 8 from the middle with gaps between them.
# · ONE MOUTH, AT THE BOTTOM, AND IT MOVED TO COL 20 to sit on the sanctum-approach's cleft. The
#   gate face of cut dark rock is re-cut around it, so the gate still stands IN a rock face.
# · THE CALDERA FLOOR IS WIDER AND THE GRASS IS A FRINGE. The knee-high grass was 37.7% of
#   walkable, above both the route figure and the town figure; the grit floor the subject line
#   also names ("mostly short grazed grass and pale volcanic grit ... fallen slabs, dry heather
#   and a few dead trees on the wide flat floor") is now given its share and the grass sits at
#   31%. That number is a reading of a tension inside the subject line itself, not a ruling, and
#   it is recorded as open.
# · THE TOP FRAME WAS EIGHT ROWS OF ONE CHARACTER — a matte panel by another name. The crater wall
#   there is now broken by the shelves the subject line puts on the high side.
# · THE PROP SITS ON THE ARRIVAL TILE and was NOT moved: data/nodes/sanctum.json puts
#   sanctum-antler-ring at native (512,512) = cell (32,32), and propAnchorToleranceNativePx admits
#   no other cell. Nothing is DRAWN there — the ring's stones stand at radius 8 and the anchor is
#   the monument's interaction point, in the middle of the grazed lawn.
#   The mechanical consequence, kept in the seeder's own words because tests/unit/mapgen.test.ts
#   requires this coincidence to be recorded in the header and never left silent: the spawn cell
#   (32,32) — native (512,512) — is also an authored prop position, so the prop-anchor character
#   keeps it and no spawn-anchor is written. The cell is walkable, which is all chooseSpawnTile
#   requires. Do not "tidy" this sentence away when re-authoring the header; it is asserted.
#
# ── legend (tools/mapgen/legend.ts) ──────────────────────────────────────────
#   .  floor  open-ground         plain walkable ground; the default fill
#   ,  floor  scrub               walkable but textured — long grass, ferns, heather
#   :  floor  path-corridor       the worn track that joins the map edges through the middle
#   o  floor  centre-clearing     the open region at native (512,512) the spawn search lands in
#   ~  floor  shallow             walkable water margin — wadeable, not a wall
#   #  WALL   wall                generic impassable mass with no more specific kind
#   B  WALL   building-footprint  a building the player walks around, never through
#   T  WALL   tree-mass           trunks and thicket dense enough to stop a walk
#   W  WALL   water               open water — deep enough to block
#   R  WALL   rock                basalt, scree, standing stone, crater rim
#   F  WALL   fence               a thin built barrier — pen rail, jetty edge, ore-cart rail
#   *  ANCH   prop-anchor         a data/nodes/*.json props[] entry, at floor(x/cellPx), floor(y/cellPx)
#   @  ANCH   spawn-anchor        documentary marker for the centre cell the spawn search starts from
#   ^  ANCH   edge-mouth          the cell where a corridor meets the map edge; one per declared adjacency
#   D  ANCH   door-anchor         the walkable cell a building is entered from
#
# Only "#"-class characters block. tools/mapgen/layout.ts normalises every WALL character to "#"
# before game/collision.ts gridFromAscii sees it; ANCH characters parse as floor and are read again
# on a second pass. Region kinds are open strings — add a row to the legend, not a case to a switch.
#
# ── what this grid uses ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
#   R  the caldera wall, the shelves on the high side, the nine standing stones, the fallen slabs
#   #  the face of cut dark rock the gate is cut into, and crags of the same rock at the cliff foot
#   T  the dead trees
#   ,  knee-high grass and dry heather — THE ENCOUNTER TERRAIN
#   .  pale volcanic grit and the worn flats — safe ground, and continuous
#   o  the grazed lawn inside the ring — safe ground
#   :  the one worn footpath
#   ^  the one edge mouth, bottom, back to sanctum-approach
#   *  sanctum-antler-ring
