# HAND-AUTHORED LAYOUT for "r5" — Duskhollow Vale. THE LAYOUT IS THE MAP DESIGN.
#
# Seeded by tools/mapgen/seed.ts, then redesigned cell by cell. The maps are composed from
# tiles now, stamped from this grid, so painting and collision correspond BY CONSTRUCTION and
# this file is the map: every boulder, every scree fan, every stand of heather is exactly where
# it is written here. Re-running the seeder OVERWRITES this; the renderers read this file.
#
# ── coordinate space ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# space        map-native pixels, origin TOP-LEFT (data/nodes/*.json props[].x/y are in this space)
# grid         64 cols × 64 rows, one character per collision cell
# cell         16 native px  (balance.map.collisionCellPx; 1024/64 = 16)
# map          1024 × 1024 native px, rendered ×2 → 2048 world px
# tiles        64 × 64 movement tiles of 32 world px
# col ← x      col = floor(nativeX / 16);  a cell's centre is native (16·col + 8, 16·row + 8)
# centre       native (512, 512) = cell (32, 32) = the tile firstWalkableTile starts on
# screen       the player sees 240×160 native = 15 × 10 CELLS at a time
#
# ── seeded from ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# biome        Vale
# adjacency    duskhollow, sanctum-approach
# bottom edge  duskhollow — ^ at (26,63), on duskhollow's top mouth; out of the vale mouth
# top edge     sanctum-approach — ^ at (40,0), on the approach's bottom mouth; the head gorge
# settlement   no — zero building footprints, zero doors
# props        r5-antler-vale @ (344,618) → cell (21,38), unmoved
# subject      tools/comfy/manifest.json _nodes.r5 — "narrow vale floor of dark blue-green
#              cropped grass, dead bracken and low heather … low pale lichen-blotched boulders,
#              scree fans off the valley sides … one still black pool lying against the foot of
#              the left-hand valley side well away from the middle. One thin pale sheep track
#              climbs the whole way up the picture, kinking round each boulder in short straight
#              runs rather than smooth curves, passing over the exact middle of the picture on
#              bare pale earth with nothing on it … Below and left of the middle one granite
#              antler stone stands quite alone, nothing but grass around it for a long way"
#
# ── decisions this file made ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
# · THE PLACE. A narrow vale under cold twilight. The track is the design: axis-aligned runs
#   joined by right-angle kinks, a boulder standing in the cell the track would have carried on
#   into at almost every turn, which is the subject line's "kinking round each boulder in short
#   straight runs rather than smooth curves" spelled as geometry. The neck at rows 18-23 closes
#   the floor to a few cells and is the only way between the lower vale and the upper bowl.
# · TWO BLOCKING MATERIALS, and the subject line asks for the distinction: "#" is the VALLEY
#   SIDES — "a change of material with a hard edge on each hand" — and "R" is kept for the LOW
#   PALE LICHEN-BLOTCHED BOULDERS on the floor and the scree fans off the sides. The map used to
#   spell all three with one character and read as the fourth grey-framed picture in a row.
# · NO PALE DISC AT THE ARRIVAL TILE. The middle is bare pale earth with nothing on it, authored.
# · THE BOULDER FIELD IS THINNED AND SIZED. Seventy-seven boulders, most of them single cells,
#   were the densest speck texture in the set. The boulders that stand in a kink are kept and
#   grown, because they are why the track turns; the rest are halved.
# · ENCOUNTER TERRAIN. 26% of walkable in seven stands in the bays either side of the track; the
#   track touches none of it, so the vale can be crossed without a forced roll.
#
# ── legend (tools/mapgen/legend.ts) ──────────────────────────────────────────
#   .  floor  open-ground         plain walkable ground; the default fill
#   ,  floor  scrub               walkable but textured — long grass, ferns, heather
#   :  floor  path-corridor       the worn track that joins the map edges through the middle
#   o  floor  centre-clearing     the open region at native (512,512) the spawn search lands in
#   ~  floor  shallow             walkable water margin — wadeable, not a wall
#   #  WALL   wall                generic impassable mass with no more specific kind
#   B  WALL   building-footprint  a building the player walks around, never through
#   T  WALL   tree-mass           trunks and thicket dense enough to stop a walk
#   W  WALL   water               open water — deep enough to block
#   R  WALL   rock                basalt, scree, standing stone, crater rim
#   F  WALL   fence               a thin built barrier — pen rail, jetty edge, ore-cart rail
#   *  ANCH   prop-anchor         a data/nodes/*.json props[] entry, at floor(x/cellPx), floor(y/cellPx)
#   @  ANCH   spawn-anchor        documentary marker for the centre cell the spawn search starts from
#   ^  ANCH   edge-mouth          the cell where a corridor meets the map edge; one per declared adjacency
#   D  ANCH   door-anchor         the walkable cell a building is entered from
#
# Only "#"-class characters block. tools/mapgen/layout.ts normalises every WALL character to "#"
# before game/collision.ts gridFromAscii sees it; ANCH characters parse as floor and are read again
# on a second pass. Region kinds are open strings — add a row to the legend, not a case to a switch.
