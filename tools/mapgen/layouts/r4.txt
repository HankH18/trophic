# HAND-AUTHORED LAYOUT for "r4" — Cinder Steppe. THIS FILE IS THE MAP.
#
# Redesigned from the layout tools/mapgen/seed.ts wrote. The maps are composed from TILES now,
# stamped cell by cell from this grid, so the painting and the collision mask correspond by
# construction and every mound, track and patch of encounter ground is exactly where it is written
# here. A boring grid here is a boring map; no tile can save it. Re-running the seeder with --force
# OVERWRITES this file; the renderers read this file, never the seeder.
#
# ── coordinate space ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
# space        map-native pixels, origin TOP-LEFT (data/nodes/*.json props[].x/y are in this space)
# grid         64 cols × 64 rows, one character per collision cell
# cell         16 native px  (balance.map.collisionCellPx; 1024/64 = 16)
# map          1024 × 1024 native px, rendered ×2 → 2048 world px
# tiles        64 × 64 movement tiles of 32 world px
# col ← x      col = floor(nativeX / 16);  a cell's centre is native (16·col + 8, 16·row + 8)
# centre       native (512, 512) = cell (32, 32) = the tile firstWalkableTile starts on
#
# ── seeded from ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# settlement   no — zero building footprints
# props        r4-antler-vent @ (596,288) → cell (37,18)
#
# ── what this place is (tools/comfy/manifest.json _nodes.r4, followed, not paraphrased) ──────
# Open volcanic steppe. Black ash flats and cracked basalt, steaming vents, loose cinder blocks and
# dry ash-grey scrub tufts. Everything on this ground is low and small, and there is no green grass
# anywhere: no tree mass, no water, no building on this map, and the layout carries none.
#
# THE TRACK. One thin ash path, almost straight up the picture, jinking ONCE — at rows 15–21, where
# it steps round the vent mound at cols 28–33. It crosses the exact middle on packed ash with
# nothing on it (row 32, cols 31–32; the '@' is at 32,32), drops toward the forge town at the
# bottom as a widened haul road, and climbs to the scarp.
#
# THE SCARP, rows 9–14. One band of dark rock lying flat across the ash, above the middle. Its
# BOTTOM LINE IS STRAIGHT AND HARD (row 14, every column) and its TOP LINE IS A ROW OF ROUNDED
# LOBES on a one-tile rhythm, of ten different widths, the two widest crowned a row higher. The ash
# is the same above the band and below it — it is a step seen from overhead, never a cutaway. ONE
# gap is cut straight through it at cols 32–34 and the track passes through, and that gap is the
# only way between the lower steppe and the ground above; the band runs off both side edges.
#
# THE RAIL. A disused narrow line ('F' is the legend's ore-cart rail): col 35, rows 45–59, with the
# lengths already lifted and carted off showing as the gaps at rows 52, 53 and 58, plus a siding
# stub along row 47. The heavy rail four of them could not shift lies near the top, row 6, cols
# 23–30, one end pointing at the track. All three are amb.r4-worker.base, which is unconditional.
#
# THE STONE. r4-antler-vent at cell (37,18) — one step east of the track, standing against the
# steaming vent at cols 38–39. Above and right of the middle, as authored, at its authored pixel.
#
# ── decisions this file made ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
# · THE PLACE. Open volcanic steppe: one thin ash track running almost straight up the picture
#   and jinking once where it steps round a vent mound, a low basalt scarp lying flat across the
#   ash with a gap cut straight through it, and the disused ore-cart rail in lengths beside the
#   track with the gaps where the crew lifted it and carted it off.
# · TWO BLOCKING MATERIALS, which is what the subject line already says: "R" is the basalt scarp
#   and the basalt shoulders, "#" is the LOOSE CINDER — the blocks and clinker banks lying on the
#   ash. The map used to be one grey material end to end, the fourth grey-rimmed picture in a row.
# · THE SCARP READS AS A BAND LYING ON THE ASH, with ash the same colour above it and below it —
#   the subject line's own words. It used to merge into the top rim on the east.
# · THE DEBRIS IS CLUSTERED, NOT SCATTERED. Sixty-one one- and two-cell blocking specks were
#   spread evenly over the whole steppe; the open flats are now open, and the debris fields sit
#   at the scarp's foot, along the rail and at the frame.
# · NO PALE DISC AT THE ARRIVAL TILE — see the note in r1's header; the same module was on six
#   of the twelve nodes. The middle is packed ash with nothing on it, which is authored.
# · THE MOUTHS ARE UNCHANGED (bottom 31, top 32) because this track is authored as running almost
#   straight. cindershelf's top mouth moved to meet it instead.
#
# ── legend (tools/mapgen/legend.ts) ──────────────────────────────────────────
#   .  floor  open-ground         plain walkable ground; the default fill
#   ,  floor  scrub               walkable but textured — long grass, ferns, heather
#   :  floor  path-corridor       the worn track that joins the map edges through the middle
#   o  floor  centre-clearing     the open region at native (512,512) the spawn search lands in
#   ~  floor  shallow             walkable water margin — wadeable, not a wall
#   #  WALL   wall                generic impassable mass with no more specific kind
#   B  WALL   building-footprint  a building the player walks around, never through
#   T  WALL   tree-mass           trunks and thicket dense enough to stop a walk
#   W  WALL   water               open water — deep enough to block
#   R  WALL   rock                basalt, scree, standing stone, crater rim
#   F  WALL   fence               a thin built barrier — pen rail, jetty edge, ore-cart rail
#   *  ANCH   prop-anchor         a data/nodes/*.json props[] entry, at floor(x/cellPx), floor(y/cellPx)
#   @  ANCH   spawn-anchor        documentary marker for the centre cell the spawn search starts from
#   ^  ANCH   edge-mouth          the cell where a corridor meets the map edge; one per declared adjacency
#   D  ANCH   door-anchor         the walkable cell a building is entered from
#

# on a second pass. Region kinds are open strings — add a row to the legend, not a case to a switch.
